About Smart Medicine Box

Smart Medicine Box is an affordable healthcare solution designed to help elderly people take medicine correctly and on time.

The product combines LED reminders, buzzer alarms, LCD display, rechargeable battery, and simple one-button operation.

Our project supports United Nations SDG 3 Good Health and Well-being.
